Gas safety at home

Simple habits that keep your kitchen safe

General guidance only. Always follow the instructions that come with your cylinder and cooker, and ask us if you're unsure.

  • Keep cylinders upright, in a well-ventilated place away from heat and flames.
  • Don't store cylinders in enclosed spaces or below ground level.
  • Replace a cracked, worn or damaged hose or regulator straight away.
  • After changing a cylinder, check the connection for leaks. Never use a flame to do it.
  • If you smell gas, turn everything off, open windows, avoid switches and flames, and move away.
